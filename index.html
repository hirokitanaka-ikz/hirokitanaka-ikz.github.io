About

I am a researcher working on crystal growth and optics and laser physics/engineering, currently particularly focusing on high-purity fluoride single crystals for efficient solid-state lasers, solid-state laser cooling, and vacuum UV nonlinear optics.

Research

Fluoride crystalsPreparation of high-purity fluoride compounds and growth and characterization of high-quality fluoride single crystals (Czochralski, Bridgman method).
Solid-state laser coolingEfficient cooling of high-purity rare-earth-dopef fluoride crystals by anti-Stokes fluorescence.
Solid-state lasersDevelopment of solid-state lasers based on novel transitions of rare-earth ions, in the UV, visible, and near-infrared spectra ranges.
Nonlinear opticsNonlinear optical crystals enabling frequency conversion to vacuum UV spectral range, where conventional oxide nonlinear crystals cannot reach.
